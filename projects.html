AstroConnect: A conceptual social network bridging Earth and orbital communities, developed as a university project for FIAP. Features include real-time geolocation, interactive orbital mapping, and a highly accessible, low-cognitive-load Dark Mode UI. Built with a modular architecture that translates space data sharing into terrestrial solutions for sustainable cities (aligning with UN SDGs 9 & 11).
